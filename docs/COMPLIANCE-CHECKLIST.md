# Compliance review checklist

This site was written to align with the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law (s133), the Ahpra/National Boards *Guidelines for advertising a regulated health service*, Australian Consumer Law and the Australian Privacy Principles. It is **not legal advice**. Before launch, the clinic owner (and ideally a qualified Australian health-advertising adviser) should work through every item.

## Advertising (National Law s133 and Ahpra guidelines)
- [ ] No testimonials or patient stories about clinical aspects anywhere on the site, social media or Google Business Profile posts the clinic controls. (None are included; Google reviews are deliberately **not** embedded.)
- [ ] No claims that create an unreasonable expectation of benefit. Check each service and condition page for wording such as "fixes", "cures", "corrects".
- [ ] No claims about correcting subluxations, restoring systemic health, or treating non-musculoskeletal conditions.
- [ ] Pregnancy and paediatric content makes no outcome claims and refers to GP/midwife/paediatrician where appropriate.
- [ ] Evidence statements (e.g. "around 90% non-specific", exercise effect sizes) checked against the cited sources.
- [ ] All six starter articles reviewed clinically, references verified, then set to *Approved*.
- [ ] Titles: "Dr" is used with "Chiropractor" alongside, so it's clear Dr Malak is not a medical practitioner. Confirm this on every page and in metadata.
- [ ] No "best chiropractor" or superiority claims; no invented awards, ratings or patient numbers.
- [ ] No time-limited offers or inducements (e.g. free consultations) without terms and Board-guideline review.
- [ ] Products page: no health claims; ingredient and allergen details verified from the manufacturer; written permission to use product imagery kept on file.

## Practitioner information
- [ ] AHPRA registration number added for Dr Malak (and Dr Tyler if his profile is published).
- [ ] Qualifications, certifications and SIRA approval verified.
- [ ] Dr Andrew Tyler: consent to be named, qualifications and bio confirmed before his profile is switched on.

## Fees, Medicare and funding (Australian Consumer Law)
- [ ] All fees entered; no hidden charges; cancellation fee stated.
- [ ] Medicare wording accurate: GP Chronic Condition Management Plan, up to 5 allied health services per calendar year shared across providers, gap stated. No rebate amounts quoted unless verified.
- [ ] DVA and SIRA "no gap" statements confirmed for the services offered.
- [ ] Provider numbers are **not** published on the public site.

## Privacy (APPs and NSW HRIP Act)
- [ ] Privacy policy completed (storage, retention, analytics, contact for complaints) and dated.
- [ ] Enquiry forms warn against sending health information (done) and deliver only to a secure, clinic-controlled inbox.
- [ ] GP referrals use a secure pathway (secure messaging or fax), not the website form.
- [ ] If analytics are added, choose a privacy-friendly tool or add consent management.
- [ ] Written consent on file from everyone who appears in website photos, including the patient in the treatment photo.

## Accessibility and safety
- [ ] Emergency notice visible in the footer and on forms (done).
- [ ] Red-flag guidance present on every condition page (done) — clinically reviewed.
