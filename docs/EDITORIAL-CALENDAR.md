## Monthly workflow (about 2–3 hours)
1. **Week 1:** create the article in the editor (Status: Draft). Write or edit the text; add references to guidelines or systematic reviews you have actually read.
2. **Week 2:** set Status to *In clinical review*. Read it as a clinician: claims supported, no promises, red flags included where relevant.
3. **Week 3:** add *Clinically reviewed by* and *Last clinically reviewed*, set Status to *Approved*, and choose a publish date. Save.
4. **Publish day:** the site rebuilds at about 6 am Sydney time; the article appears automatically. Share it on LinkedIn/Instagram.
5. **Every 12 months:** re-review older articles and update the review date.

> AI drafting tools can help with a first draft, but nothing reaches the site until a chiropractor has reviewed and approved it — the website enforces this.
